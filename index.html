    How we calculate

    This is an estimate for public education, not certified emissions accounting. Here is every step, so you can check it.

    1. Yearly miles = one-way miles × 2 × days per week × weeks.
    2. Car CO2 = yearly miles ÷ your mpg × lbs of CO2 per gallon of gasoline.
    3. Your ride's CO2 = yearly miles × the passenger-mile factor for your mode, below.
    4. CO2 avoided = car CO2 minus your ride's CO2.
    5. Gas money = yearly miles ÷ mpg × gas price.

    What this leaves out

    • Transit factors are national averages. A full bus or an electrified line on a clean grid does better; a nearly empty one does worse. Agency-specific factors are planned.
    • Car numbers count only the tailpipe, not making the car, the fuel supply chain, or parking. Real savings from leaving the car are usually larger.
    • Electric cars, carpools, and fares aren't modeled yet.
    • Tree seedlings use EPA's figure for one urban tree grown for 10 years. Phone charges use EPA's per-charge figure.
